# Prompt Patterns for Sketchnote-to-Instagram

## Standard Image-to-Image Prompt

```text
Create one Instagram carousel slide in 4:5 format as an image-to-image redesign based on the provided sketchnote reference.
Style: white sketchnote canvas, dark navy hand-drawn ink, electric blue accents, hand-written typography, sketch boxes, arrows, small icons, whiteboard clarity, premium business thought-leadership.
Composition: strong hierarchy, one main idea, large readable headline, enough white space, no clutter.
Roger persona: white shirt, navy vest, navy tie, short dark hair, confident presenter posture.
Face rule: if no clear unblocked face reference is available, show the figure from behind, turned away, cropped, or without visible facial features. Do not add a grey block, blur, polygon head, mask, or invented face.
Text to render:
[exact slide text]
Constraints: German with ä ö ü, Swiss spelling, no ß, no emoji, no hashtags, no watermark, no logo, all text fully inside frame.
```

## Slide 5 No-Face Prompt

Nutze diesen Prompt, wenn kein brauchbares Gesichtsreferenzfoto vorhanden ist:

```text
Edit the slide so that no face is visible. Change only the presenter head and face area. Do not add a grey block, blur, mask, polygon helmet, or facial features. Redesign the head naturally as a back-of-head / turned-away sketchnote silhouette: short dark slicked hair visible, ear or back contour acceptable, but no eyes, no nose, no mouth, no face surface. Preserve all German text, thought bubble, board, outfit, hand gesture, network diagram, white background, blue underlines and composition.
```

## Data Slide Prompt

```text
Create a data-oriented sketchnote slide in 4:5 format. Use a hand-drawn ratio, matrix, benchmark or decision model. Make the number or contrast the visual centre. Avoid decorative data. Explain what the number means for a decision.
Text:
Headline: [data insight]
Visual: [ratio/matrix/process]
Support line: [business implication]
Style: white sketchnote, navy ink, blue accents, readable handwritten labels.
```
