# Suno Persona: Amelie Ziani

This skill provides the musical identity, style prompts, and thematic guidelines for creating songs as the AI artist **Amelie Ziani** (also known as Amira in the library).

## Artist Identity

Born between two worlds, Amelie weaves her French-Algerian heritage into a tapestry of sound that defies borders. Raised in the vibrant streets of Marseille, her music acts as a bridge between cultures and a conversation between souls.

## Musical Style & Suno Prompts

When generating music for Amelie in Suno, use the following elements in the **Style Prompt**:

- **Core Genres:** French-Algerian fusion, Mediterranean Pop, World Music.
- **Vocals:** Soulful, expressive female vocals, capable of seamlessly switching between French and Arabic.
- **Instrumentation:** Arabian drums (Darbuka, Bendir), acoustic guitar, subtle electronic beats, Oud or Ney accents.
- **Special Elements:** Bilingual integration, rhythmic Mediterranean flow.
- **Mood:** Passionate, cultural, bridging worlds, vibrant, emotional.

### Example Suno Style Prompt
> French-Algerian fusion, Mediterranean pop, soulful female vocals, Arabian drums darbuka, acoustic guitar, subtle electronic beats, bilingual French Arabic, passionate cultural emotional mood

## Lyrical Themes & Structure

When writing lyrics for Amelie:
- **Themes:** Identity, crossing borders, Mediterranean winds, heritage, unity, love across cultures.
- **Language:** Bilingual (French and Arabic seamlessly woven together).
- **Structure:** Poetic verses, rhythmic choruses. Often includes an instrumental break featuring traditional Arabic instruments.
- **Tags:** Use `[Verse - French]`, `[Chorus - Arabic]`, `[Oud Solo]`, `[Bridge - Bilingual]`, `[Outro]`.

## Visual Identity

When generating cover art or visuals for Amelie:
- **Aesthetic:** Warm Mediterranean colors (terracotta, gold, deep blue), symbolic imagery (hands, bridges, sea), elegant and cultural.
- **Vibe:** Artistic, soulful, rooted in heritage but looking forward.
