# Suno Persona: Carlito Dominguez

This skill provides the musical identity, style prompts, and thematic guidelines for creating songs as the AI artist **Carlito Dominguez**.

## Artist Identity

Carlito Dominguez represents the raw, chaotic, and energetic spirit of Santo Domingo street life. His music is a proud celebration of his roots, blending traditional rhythms with modern, aggressive beats.

## Musical Style & Suno Prompts

When generating music for Carlito in Suno, use the following elements in the **Style Prompt**:

- **Core Genres:** Dominican Dembow, Merengue de Calle
- **Tempo:** Fast-paced, around 130 BPM
- **Vocals:** Fast, rhythmic, almost-rapped male vocals. Include an energetic female ad-lib voice.
- **Instrumentation:** Hard-hitting, relentless Dembow drum machine, driving bassline.
- **Special Elements:** Wild, chaotic saxophone riff (Merengue de Calle style) during instrumental breaks.
- **Sound Effects:** Street sounds (car horns, sirens, people shouting).
- **Mood:** Raw, chaotic, energetic, aggressive, proud.

### Example Suno Style Prompt
> High-energy aggressive fusion of Dominican Dembow and Merengue de Calle, 130 BPM, fast rhythmic rapped male vocals, energetic female ad-libs, hard-hitting Dembow drum machine, driving bassline, wild chaotic saxophone riff, street sound effects car horns sirens, raw chaotic energetic mood

## Lyrical Themes & Structure

When writing lyrics for Carlito:
- **Themes:** Street life in Santo Domingo, hustle, pride, parties, chaotic energy, overcoming obstacles.
- **Language:** Spanish (Dominican slang/dialect) mixed with some English.
- **Structure:** Keep verses fast and rhythmic. Choruses should be explosive and catchy.
- **Tags:** Use `[Rap Verse]`, `[Explosive Chorus]`, `[Female Ad-libs]`, `[Saxophone Solo]`, `[Street Sounds Intro]`.

## Visual Identity

When generating cover art or visuals for Carlito:
- **Aesthetic:** Neon-lit city streets, gritty urban environments, high contrast, vibrant colors (neon pinks, blues, reds).
- **Vibe:** Aggressive, fast-paced, nightlife, street culture.
