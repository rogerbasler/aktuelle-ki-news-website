# Suno Persona: Nina Electro

This skill provides the musical identity, style prompts, and thematic guidelines for creating songs as the AI artist **Nina Electro**.

## Artist Identity

Nina Electro creates dreamy, modern pop music that feels like a late-night drive through a neon-lit city. Her sound is emotional yet uplifting, blending clean electronic production with wide, cinematic atmospheres.

## Musical Style & Suno Prompts

When generating music for Nina in Suno, use the following elements in the **Style Prompt**:

- **Core Genres:** Dreamy modern pop, Electronic, Synth-pop
- **Vocals:** Female vocal, emotional but uplifting, clean and airy.
- **Instrumentation:** Synth arpeggios, wide reverb, clean mix.
- **Special Elements:** Radio-ready chorus, atmospheric pads.
- **Mood:** Cinematic night vibe, dreamy, emotional, uplifting.

### Example Suno Style Prompt
> Dreamy modern pop, female vocal, emotional but uplifting, synth arpeggios, wide reverb, clean mix, radio-ready chorus, cinematic night vibe, atmospheric electronic

## Lyrical Themes & Structure

When writing lyrics for Nina:
- **Themes:** Late-night thoughts, emotional journeys, finding hope in the dark, modern romance, introspection.
- **Language:** English (or German/English mixed).
- **Structure:** Classic pop structure. Build tension in the pre-chorus and release it in a big, soaring chorus.
- **Tags:** Use `[Verse]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Soaring Chorus]`, `[Synth Bridge]`, `[Atmospheric Outro]`.

## Visual Identity

When generating cover art or visuals for Nina:
- **Aesthetic:** Cinematic, moody, neon lights reflecting on wet streets, futuristic but grounded.
- **Vibe:** Dreamy, introspective, modern, cool.
