# #ThinkRoger Bright Purple Editorial Design

## Principle

The carousel should feel like an international editorial cover translated into an Instagram sequence. It is constructive, intelligent, visually decisive, and optimistic without being naïve. The design creates tension through scale, contrast, and depth, but uses a vivid Bright Purple to signal insight, value creation, and forward-looking solutions while remaining clearly distinct from pink or electric blue.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | `#000000` | Default canvas for maximum contrast |
| Secondary surface | `#080B10` | Graphite depth and image overlays |
| Primary text | `#FFFFFF` | Main thesis and essential text |
| Secondary text | `#C7CED8` | Short context lines only |
| Primary accent | `#8B5CF6` | Bright Purple: One keyword, number, or decision cue per slide; use for large emphasis and decisive visual signals |
| Quiet accent | `#C4B5FD` | Soft Violet for small labels, source markers and restrained highlights only |

Do not use electric blue, pink, warm neon colours, rainbow gradients, or multicolour data visualisations without an evidence-driven reason. The primary accent MUST be Bright Purple `#8B5CF6`. Use Soft Violet `#C4B5FD` only for small labels or subtle highlights, never as the dominant headline colour.

## Accent Visibility Rule

Use Bright Purple `#8B5CF6` on at least one large headline word, number, or decision cue per slide. The accent should occupy approximately 8-20% of the visible canvas so it reads clearly on mobile. Keep it saturated and luminous against black; do not render the main accent as dark plum, burgundy, magenta, lavender, or a low-contrast purple shadow. Use `#C4B5FD` only for secondary labels and small source markers.

## Typography

Use Avenir Next where available and Barlow Condensed as the fallback. Headlines should be massive and compressed, with clean positive line spacing. Prefer 1-4 words per line. Shorten text before reducing font size.

| Text Role | 1080×1350 Guidance |
|---|---|
| Hero thesis | 150-210px |
| Main headline | 120-185px |
| Framework item | 58-82px |
| Supporting line | 34-48px |
| Source label | 22-30px |

Maintain at least 80px of safe margin. The main statement should occupy roughly 45-70% of the visible composition.

## Image Logic

Slides 1, 2, 3, and 9 require strong, concrete visual motifs. A motif must have a subject, a decision, a solution, or a system relationship. Prefer human agency, institutional power, evidence, successful trade-offs, interfaces with positive consequences, and recognisable physical metaphors.

Avoid generic robots, glowing brains, floating code, random neon tunnels, empty dashboards, decorative circuit boards, or anonymous holograms. These are visual shorthand for "technology" but rarely explain the argument. 

## Slide-Specific Visual Functions

| Slide | Visual Function |
|---|---|
| 1 THESIS | The strongest pattern interruption, expressed as a constructive insight or opportunity. |
| 2 WHY NOW | The trigger, event, shift, document, signal, or threshold. |
| 3 EVIDENCE | The source, measurement, documented case, scale, or comparison. |
| 4 MECHANISM | A process, causal chain, or interaction between components that creates value. |
| 5 HIDDEN OPPORTUNITY | A blind spot, overlooked advantage, hidden dependency, or delayed benefit. |
| 6 COUNTERPOINT | A credible limitation, alternative explanation, or boundary condition. |
| 7 SYSTEM VIEW | Actors, incentives, dependencies, and feedback loops. |
| 8 THINKING SHIFT | A clear change in perspective with strong typographic focus. |
| 9 FRAMEWORK | A decision gate, diagnostic, control room, map, or three-part test. |
| 10 THINK AGAIN | Restrained brand close and one clear constructive action. |

## Forbidden Elements

Do not use thin dividers, underlines, spray-paint separators, rounded SaaS cards, tiny body copy, cluttered graffiti, fake interface data, invented charts, or decorative citations. Never place text outside safe zones or allow text lines to overlap.

## Prompt Core

```text
Generate one premium Instagram carousel slide in exact 4:5 ratio (1080x1350px) for #ThinkRoger.
Style: dark cinematic international editorial poster about technology, opportunity, business and human agency.
Background: pure black or deep graphite with realistic depth, restrained film grain, vignette and sharp light edges.
Typography: massive uppercase Avenir Next or Barlow Condensed, clean positive line spacing, 1-4 words per line, fully readable on mobile, 80px safe margin.
Colours: white text with one high-visibility Bright Purple #8B5CF6 emphasis. Soft Violet #C4B5FD is allowed only for small labels or source markers. No electric blue, no pink, no rainbow palette.
Composition: one central idea, one concrete visual motif, no generic AI imagery, no thin dividers, no rounded cards, no decorative dashboard.
The result must feel like a serious, constructive magazine cover and keynote poster, not a worksheet or an advertisement.
```

Append the slide-specific purpose, exact text, and visual motif to this prompt. When a generated image contains incorrect text, use it as the visual base and rebuild typography deterministically rather than accepting errors.
