# #ThinkRoger Dark Cinematic Editorial Design

## Principle

The carousel should feel like an international editorial cover translated into an Instagram sequence. It is dark, cinematic, intelligent, and visually decisive. The design creates tension through scale, contrast, image depth, and negative space, not through decorative noise.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | `#000000` | Default canvas |
| Secondary surface | `#080B10` | Graphite depth and image overlays |
| Primary text | `#FFFFFF` | Main thesis and essential text |
| Secondary text | `#C7CED8` | Short context lines only |
| Primary accent | `#00AAFF` | One keyword, number, or decision cue per slide |
| Quiet accent | `#4A7FA1` | Labels, source markers, restrained glow |

Do not use pink, warm neon colours, rainbow gradients, or multicolour data visualisations without an evidence-driven reason.

## Typography

Use Avenir Next where available and Barlow Condensed as the fallback. Headlines should be massive and compressed, with clean positive line spacing. Prefer 1-4 words per line. Shorten text before reducing font size.

| Text Role | 1080×1350 Guidance |
|---|---|
| Hero thesis | 150-210px |
| Main headline | 120-185px |
| Framework item | 58-82px |
| Supporting line | 34-48px |
| Source label | 22-30px |

Maintain at least 80px of safe margin. The main statement should occupy roughly 45-70% of the visible composition. Thesis and Thinking Shift slides may dominate further if readability remains intact.

## Image Logic

Slides 1, 2, 3, and 9 require strong, concrete visual motifs. A motif must have a subject, a decision, a tension, or a system relationship. Prefer human agency, institutional power, evidence, trade-offs, interfaces with consequences, and recognisable physical metaphors.

Avoid generic robots, glowing brains, floating code, random neon tunnels, empty dashboards, decorative circuit boards, or anonymous blue holograms. These are visual shorthand for "technology" but rarely explain the argument. Pretty fog is still fog.

## Slide-Specific Visual Functions

| Slide | Visual Function |
|---|---|
| 1 THESIS | The strongest pattern interruption, expressed as a concrete conflict or paradox. |
| 2 WHY NOW | The trigger, event, shift, document, signal, or threshold. |
| 3 EVIDENCE | The source, measurement, documented case, scale, or comparison. |
| 4 MECHANISM | A process, causal chain, or interaction between components. |
| 5 HIDDEN ASSUMPTION | A blind spot, excluded variable, hidden dependency, or delayed cost. |
| 6 COUNTERPOINT | A credible benefit, alternative explanation, or boundary condition. |
| 7 SYSTEM VIEW | Actors, incentives, dependencies, and feedback loops. |
| 8 THINKING SHIFT | A clear change in perspective with strong typographic focus. |
| 9 FRAMEWORK | A decision gate, diagnostic, control room, map, or three-part test. |
| 10 THINK AGAIN | Restrained brand close and one clear action. |

## Forbidden Elements

Do not use thin dividers, underlines, spray-paint separators, rounded SaaS cards, tiny body copy, cluttered graffiti, fake interface data, invented charts, or decorative citations. Never place text outside safe zones or allow text lines to overlap.

## Prompt Core

```text
Generate one premium Instagram carousel slide in exact 4:5 ratio (1080x1350px) for #ThinkRoger.
Style: dark cinematic international editorial poster about technology, power, business and human agency.
Background: pure black or deep graphite with realistic depth, restrained film grain, vignette and sharp light edges.
Typography: massive uppercase Avenir Next or Barlow Condensed, clean positive line spacing, 1-4 words per line, fully readable on mobile, 80px safe margin.
Colours: white text with one Electric Blue #00aaff emphasis. No pink, no rainbow palette.
Composition: one central idea, one concrete visual motif, no generic AI imagery, no thin dividers, no rounded cards, no decorative dashboard.
The result must feel like a serious magazine cover and keynote poster, not a worksheet or an advertisement.
```

Append the slide-specific purpose, exact text, and visual motif to this prompt. When a generated image contains incorrect text, use it as the visual base and rebuild typography deterministically rather than accepting errors.
