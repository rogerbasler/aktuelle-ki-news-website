# White Cyberpunk Graffiti Image Design

Use this skill to create **image-based presentation slides** with a bright, white cyberpunk-graffiti aesthetic. The goal is not a corporate white deck with neon stickers. The goal is a high-impact visual keynote system: **white canvas, black precision, neon tension, raw graffiti energy and AI-generated key visuals**.

This skill is optimised for decks generated as full-slide images through ChatGPT Image or Nano Banana-style generation. Use it when visual impact matters more than editability.

## Core Principle

Create slides as **visual statements**, not documents. Each slide should communicate one idea in three seconds and leave space for Roger to speak the nuance.

> The slide is the punch. Roger is the argument. Do not make the slide explain the whole thought like it has abandonment issues.

## Design DNA

The visual language combines a clean white canvas with deliberate visual rebellion. Keep the white dominant. Neon accents should feel like sprayed signals, not confetti.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Background | Mostly white, off-white or very light concrete, never grey corporate sludge |
| Contrast | Hard black typography and ink-like structural elements |
| Accents | Electric blue, neon pink and lime green used sparingly |
| Texture | Graffiti spray, paint splatter, scratches, tape, photocopy noise, scanner artefacts |
| Cyberpunk | Data lines, holographic fragments, terminal glow, AI grids, neural patterns |
| Mood | Bright, sharp, rebellious, intelligent, keynote-ready |
| Composition | One dominant metaphor, strong negative space, clear text area |

## Typography Direction

Use **huge black uppercase headlines** with a graffiti-display attitude. The font should feel hand-cut, sprayed, scratched or stencil-inspired, while staying readable on stage. Do not write font names onto the slides. If text is generated inside images, prompt for **bold readable uppercase graffiti-stencil typography**.

| Text layer | Visual behaviour |
|---|---|
| Headline | Massive, black, uppercase, graffiti-stencil feel, high readability |
| Highlight word | Neon-pink or electric-blue sprayed underline, circle or strike |
| Micro label | Small terminal-style tag, only if needed |
| Body copy | Avoid wherever possible; use spoken explanation instead |

## Slide Format

Default to **16:9 landscape** for keynotes and business presentations. Use **image mode** when creating slide decks. Each slide should be generated as a full-slide image and then presented as a deck.

Use at most **12 slides by default** unless the user requests more. For short keynotes, 8 to 12 slides are usually stronger than a long gallery of semi-useful visuals. Yes, restraint is also a feature.

## Prompt Formula for Full Slide Images

Write image prompts in English for best generation quality. Use this structure for every slide image.

```text
Create a 16:9 keynote slide image in a white cyberpunk graffiti style.

Title text to render: "[SHORT UPPERCASE HEADLINE]"
Optional subline: "[MAXIMUM ONE SHORT LINE]"

Visual metaphor: [one concrete image idea].
Scene: bright white concrete or paper-like stage, black ink structures, cyberpunk data fragments, neon accents.
Composition: [where the title sits, where the visual object sits, where negative space remains].
Typography: huge readable black uppercase graffiti-stencil headline, sprayed neon highlight on one key word, no tiny text.
Colour: dominant white background, black structure, one accent colour [blue/pink/lime].
Texture: subtle spray paint, torn paper, scanner noise, scratches, tape marks.
Constraints: no logos, no watermarks, no unreadable microtext, no corporate stock style, no clutter.
```

## Prompt Formula for Textless Backgrounds

Use this when the slide will be edited later in Canva, PowerPoint or Keynote.

```text
Create a 16:9 textless keynote background in a white cyberpunk graffiti style.

Visual metaphor: [one concrete image idea].
Scene: bright white concrete or paper-like stage, black ink structures, cyberpunk data fragments and sparse neon accents.
Composition: leave a clear high-contrast empty area for later headline overlay, preferably left third or upper third.
Motion readiness: include particles, data lines, spray textures or light sources that could later be animated.
Constraints: absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no captions, no readable UI, no watermarks.
```

## Prompt Formula for Animated Backgrounds

Use this when turning a generated background into an MP4 loop.

```text
Animate the provided 16:9 white cyberpunk graffiti keynote background into a subtle stage-ready MP4.
Keep the exact composition. Do not add text, letters, numbers, captions, logos or readable UI.
Use slow cinematic parallax, gentle light breathing, sparse neon particle drift and subtle data-line shimmer.
Keep the main subject stable and preserve the empty text area for later overlay.
The motion should support a live speaker, not compete with them.
No cuts, no camera shake, no excessive motion, no audio.
```

## Quality Rules

Every generated slide must pass these checks.

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| One idea | The slide says one thing only. |
| Readability | Title is readable from the back of a room. |
| White dominance | The slide still feels bright and white, not dark mode with white corners. |
| Graffiti restraint | Spray textures support the idea, they do not become wallpaper. |
| Cyberpunk relevance | Data, grids and neon signals serve the message. |
| Speaker support | The slide gives Roger room to speak. |
| No clutter | No tiny UI noise, no fake labels, no decorative mess. |

## Absolute Rules

Do not create corporate-clean slides when this skill is active. Do not overload slides with paragraphs. Do not generate small unreadable pseudo-text. Do not use logos unless the user provides them and explicitly requests their use. Do not place font names on slides. Do not add QR codes unless requested. Do not make every slide look the same; preserve the design DNA while varying composition.
